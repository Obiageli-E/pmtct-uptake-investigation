# Investigating Low PMTCT Service Uptake Among Pregnant Women in Nigerian Communities

My individual project for the WALUAIS Batch III Fellowship, under the Women Ascension Learning and Understanding of AI and STEAM Department.

## What this is

Some pregnant women may not use PMTCT (Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV) services even when the services are available. I wanted to understand why before thinking about a solution. I used systems thinking to list possible reasons, picked transportation to test, and used AI to help spot patterns. I then checked every result against the data myself.

## What I found

In a small exercise with 8 sample responses, 5 said transportation had made it hard to attend a visit, and 4 said they had missed or delayed one.

## Please note

The 8 responses are sample data created for a learning exercise. They did not come from real women, so these results do not show that transportation is a major barrier. They show what is worth testing next with a proper sample.
